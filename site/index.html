Method

The unit: expected remaining range

A trading day runs 18:00 to 17:00 New York time. Each market’s typical daily range is tracked with a 20-day exponential average. The volatility profile measures how much of a day’s movement each hour usually carries, so at any moment the engine knows how much range is still to come before the 16:10 flat time of the MyFundedFutures account it is fitted to. Levels are expressed as fractions of that remaining range.

Touch odds

For every hour of every past day, the engine measured how far price went above and below that hour’s close before 16:00, in remaining-range units. Those distributions, kept separately for up and down moves, become the odds for any level you check. Late in the day, with only a bar or two left, moves fall short of what a single distribution expects, so each hour can get its own (pooled with the hours either side). That is used only where it beats the shared version out of sample: so far for NQ, not gold.

Pace

A fast day tends to stay fast. Today’s range so far, compared with what the profile expected by now, stretches or shrinks the remaining range. This adjustment is used only because it beat the plain model out of sample in both halves of the test.

Strategy lab

Every strategy was registered on 19 November 2026 with fixed rules, before any was run, and each is tested on both markets. Intraday rules decide on completed hourly bars, enter at the next bar’s open, check stops before targets inside a bar and are flat at the 16:00 bar close, inside the account’s 16:10 auto-close. Daily rules trade close to close; they hold overnight, so the 50K Builder doesn’t allow them and they’re kept as research. Costs are about one tick of slippage each side plus commission. To pass, a rule needs a t-statistic past one Bonferroni bar across every test, must beat 95% of 200 runs of the same rule at random times, and must be profitable in both halves of its history and over the last year. Each test also reports the smallest edge its data could detect, so a rule that couldn’t be judged is labelled inconclusive rather than failed. Intraday history: Dukascopy hourly mid prices (gold from 2010, NQ from 2018) until mid-2024, futures bars after. Trades after the registration date form a forward record the rules could not have been tuned on.

What failed

No hour of the day closes up more often than chance once you account for testing every hour in both markets. The desk shows those tests but leans on none of them. The opening-range statistics are still collecting: there are only about 60 days of free 5-minute bars, and they need 250 before they count.
